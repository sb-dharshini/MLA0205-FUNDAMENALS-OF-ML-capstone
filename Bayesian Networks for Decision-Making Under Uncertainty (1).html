💡 Introduction

Reasoning about things we are not sure of.

1

Graphical models

Bayesian Networks represent relationships between uncertain variables.

2

Probability-driven

They use probability theory to analyze evidence and estimate possible outcomes.

3

Adaptive

Predictions are updated when new information becomes available.

4

Web-based

The website shows probability estimates, risk levels and decision recommendations.

⚠️ Problem Statement

Why a new approach is needed.

Incomplete information

Decision-making becomes difficult when information is incomplete or uncertain.

Hidden dependencies

Traditional methods may not effectively represent dependencies between multiple factors.

Need to update

A system is needed that can update probabilities based on new evidence.

Our answer

Use Bayesian Networks to estimate outcomes and support informed decisions.

🎯 Objectives

What the project sets out to do.

1

Construct a Bayesian Network representing relationships between uncertain variables.

2

Calculate prior and conditional probabilities.

3

Update posterior probabilities when new evidence is provided.

4

Estimate outcome probabilities and assess risk.

5

Recommend suitable decisions using expected utility.

6

Develop an interactive website for probabilistic decision support.

🧩 Modules

Three building blocks of the proposed system.

1

Bayesian Network Construction

  • Identify the important input variables.
  • Define relationships using a directed acyclic graph (DAG).
  • Determine priors and conditional probability tables (CPTs).
  • Establish the dependency structure.
  • Prepare the network for inference.
2

Probabilistic Inference

  • Accept evidence and input values from the user.
  • Apply Bayesian calculations to update beliefs.
  • Calculate posterior probabilities for outcomes.
  • Estimate likelihood of favorable and unfavorable events.
  • Evaluate performance using metrics and test data.
3

Website & Decision Support

  • Build the site with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Implement the inference engine with Python and Flask.
  • Let users enter evidence through input forms.
  • Show probabilities and risk levels in charts.
  • Recommend actions by expected utility, tested on sample cases.

🏁 Conclusion

The proposed system provides a structured approach to decision-making under uncertainty.

Bayesian Networks analyze dependencies and update probabilities based on evidence.

The website presents outcome probabilities, risk assessments and decision recommendations.

Future work: learning probabilities from real datasets, improving inference efficiency, and validating the model across different applications.
